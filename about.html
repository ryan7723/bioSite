About Me

Matthew Peifer is a deeply passionate person who cares about the arts and helping those with disabilities. He loves all forms of art, paintings, drawings, and digital art. This includes other forms of art, such as music and video games. When he is not attending art shows, he is also a gamer. He is very invested in virtual reality gaming, the cool things you can do in these games, and their user interface design. When he is not doing either of these, he likes to go rock climbing. Not to be confused with outdoor rock climbing, but indoor rock climbing. The rock climbing he does is called bouldering: you are not attached to any safety gear, but there is a crash mat below you if you fall, and the climbs are at a safe height.

A graduate of the Rochester Institute of Technology, he wants to utilize his degree in computer science to create websites for companies that provide accessibility and functionality for people with disabilities. He believes websites should have simple, user-friendly designs for everyone, especially those with disabilities. While in college, he learned sign language to help people with hearing disabilities. He believes everyone deserves to use the website, including people with disabilities. “The library is available to everyone, so the internet should be as well.”

He is currently working two jobs to build up enough funds to move to New York City and live there. Finding a job in the technical and computer field is difficult where he currently lives, so once he is able to move out and support himself, he will be able to find more job opportunities aligned with his goals. It has been difficult since graduating, but the one thing that motivates him the most is his cat Noodle, whom he loves very much and looks to as a reason to keep going.
